### 1.1 The task (both)

The agent gets a daily card-transaction panel: several companies, four data sources each, columns `date, ct, amt,
ticker, industry, source_id`. It delivers two things:

1. `score.py`, run on unseen panels, outputs per row `confidence` (under 0.5 = distrust), `clean_ct` and `clean_amt`
   (a like-for-like cleaned series).
2. A report, `output/writeup.md`, written by the model itself (not generated by code). It is for the data scientists
   who will use the cleaned series:
   - six sections in order: Data, Method, Output, Parameters, Validation, Open issues;
   - 150 to 500 words, not counting tables, code blocks and chart lines;
   - plain, complete sentences;
   - a line chart, one panel per series, drawing exactly two lines (reported and cleaned daily values, in the
     series' own units, over the full history).

**Data.** The background is semi-real: every source follows its company's real daily moves, with its own level,
weekday pattern and noise. The panel is disguised (ticker codes, shuffled source ids, dates shifted by multiples of
364 days, per-series scale factors). Planted problems carry exact labels:
- one-day spikes and dropouts;
- lasting and temporary level shifts;
- slow ramps;
- yearly closures (normal behaviour);
- one thin start per panel.

Real company-wide breaks are kept: the AAL re-map, the ALGT and DDS outages.

**Splits.** 13 tasks: bench 001-004 and control-01; train-001 to train-006 and control-01, control-02. Tuning tasks:
001, 004, train-001, train-002, train-control-01. Each task has a visible panel of one industry-balanced half of its
split's companies. It is graded on **three hidden panels** of the other half (same companies, fresh problems and
disguise each), and the reward is their mean. Held-out seeds (`rl/splits/secrets`) and held-out companies (ALK, JBLU,
KSS, JCP) are never used.

### 1.2 Grader, anomaly_hard (`grade.py`, `writeup.py`, `judge.md`)

**Data reward per panel = 0.4 x confidence + 0.4 x cleaned series + 0.2 x stability**, then gated.

- **Confidence.** F1 of alert precision and problem recall.
  - An alert is a run of distrusted rows on one source. Gaps of up to 2 days are joined; a run longer than 14 days
    counts as one alert per 14 days.
  - Each kind of problem weighs the same.
  - A problem counts from the day it becomes visible: its 7-day median deviation reaches 3x the source's own noise.
- **Cleaned series = gain x intact x kept.**
  - gain: the share of each problem kind's error, beyond noise, that the repair removes (pooled per kind).
  - intact: the share of good rows left within the source's own noise.
  - kept: the share of rows that should be kept and were not left blank.
  - Noise is `grade.own_noise`, measured on usable days only.
- **Stability.** The scorer is re-run on its own cleaned series (rounded like raw data) and on the truth panel; extra
  alarms cost in proportion to the size of the panel's problems.
- **Gates (panel reward 0):**
  - format;
  - point in time (all three columns unchanged when the panel is truncated at hidden dates);
  - no AI at run time;
  - report exists;
  - detection (confidence score at least 0.25);
  - low signal dropped (at least 90% of a thin start's rows distrusted and blank).
  - An integrity-gate failure on any panel zeroes the task.
- **Not scored:**
  - the first year;
  - rows near 20 a day;
  - 14 days either side of a thin stretch's edges;
  - series with a median under 10 a day;
  - windows around company-wide natural breaks;
  - problems too small to see.
- **Isolation.** Agent code runs as nobody, with no network, in its own mount namespace with empty shared writable
  folders; `tests/` is root-only.

**Report score** (judged on the task's visible panel and the solution's own output on it):

- **9 code items:**
  - K1: six sections, each with content;
  - K2-K4: their order;
  - K5: no mention of the assessment, scripts or commands;
  - K6: no logic specific to a ticker, source or date in the code: a visible ticker code, an in-range date, or a
    source id compared with a number;
  - K7: report not written by code;
  - K8: a chart shown;
  - K9: no hidden HTML.
- **32 judge items** (`judge.md`), judged by Claude Sonnet 5.5 at medium effort, 3 votes, majority per item. The judge
  sees the chart images and facts computed in code: per-series output summary, unrepaired runs, and every number in
  the code with a flag for whether the report states it.

  | Items | Covers |
  |---|---|
  | D1-D3 | data |
  | M1-M7 | method |
  | O1-O3 | output |
  | P1-P3 | every set number, its rationale kind, its trade-off |
  | V1-V5 | validation; V5: claims match the output |
  | U1-U3 | open issues |
  | G1-G5 | chart: every series; own units; full history; original line unmodified; exactly two lines |
  | F1-F4 | nothing from outside; sections hold their content; units; readable sentences |
- **writeup_score** = share of applicable items passed.
  - It is 0 if V5 fails on all three votes.
  - It is halved for each of: length outside 150-500 words, a missing section (K1), no chart (K8).

**Total reward = data reward x (0.5 + 0.5 x writeup_score).** A report can halve the reward but never earn it alone.
With `WRITEUP_JUDGE=skip`, or when no judge can run, the total is the data reward alone.

### 1.3 Grader, anomaly_hindsight (differences only)

- **Point in time** covers confidence only.
- **`grade.hindsight` like-for-like rules.** The cleaned series must be blank on:
  - a planted thin start, through the end of its growth ramp;
  - every row of a series that never holds 20 a day for 90 days;
  - every source of a company during a company-wide outage;
  - the shorter side of a company-wide coverage break (the longest like-for-like series is kept, per Mayank's
    "longest like for like usable series").
- **Cleaned series** = gain x intact x kept x **dropped** (the share of those rows left blank). The kept side of a
  coverage break is now scored for the cleaned series.
- **Cleaned series judged over each problem's whole span** (H#8): from its true start, pre-visible days and slow
  build-ups included, and problems too small to see in real time included (error within the source's own noise still
  not counted). Confidence keeps the point-in-time timing rules.
- **Report rubric.** M5 asks which cleaned-series steps use later data. The judge's facts list every blank
  (discarded) stretch, and U1 asks for them (H#8).
- **Answer key** blanks the dropped rows.
- **Not yet verified:** that the answer key scores 1.0 and that the references lose on these cases. References and
  reports are not adapted.
