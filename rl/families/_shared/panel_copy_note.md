This copy of the panel is disguised. Tickers are random codes and source ids are shuffled. Dates are shifted by a whole
multiple of 364 days, so weekdays line up but holidays can sit a few days off their usual dates. Each ticker-source
series has its `ct` and `amt` multiplied by its own hidden factor between 0.3 and 3, so levels are not comparable across
series. Growth within a series and average ticket size (`amt` / `ct`) are real.
