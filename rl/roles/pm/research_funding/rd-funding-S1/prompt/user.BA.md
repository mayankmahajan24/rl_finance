Who this is for:

A mid-size fundamental long/short equity fund. ~30 investment staff, a three-person data team that licenses and cleans alt-data panels, and one engineer. Horizon is six to eighteen months. They can run a panel study or a cohort analysis and routinely buy transaction and web data, but they do not have tick data or execution infrastructure and will not build production ML. Output is expected to change position sizing on named companies, and to be defensible to a PM who will ask what would have to be true for it to be wrong.

Research question:

Do customers who start trading on Kalshi subsequently reduce their spend and activity on DraftKings? We have a credit-card and bank-deposit transaction panel covering both.

---

PLAN A:

**Hypothesis.** H1: conditional on total speculative wallet, a within-user rise in Kalshi share `K/(K+DK+FD)` cuts DK deposits that month and the next by more than the mechanical amount. H2: category substitution drops FD too; DK down, FD up is brand switching. H3: flat total wallet with rotating composition = cannibalization, rising total = expansion. H4: displacement concentrated in long-tenured high-spend users, not new low-spend ones.

**Data.** User-month card-and-bank panel, Jan 2023–latest complete month; full outflow ledger with consumption. Hand-audited descriptor dictionary: Kalshi variants; DK sportsbook/DFS/casino separate columns summed for headline; likewise FD; 500-transaction drift audit. Two attribution gaps quantified: broker-distributed Kalshi (Robinhood, Webull) has no descriptor: test adopter concentration among non-brokerage users, report the undercount; sportsbooks' own late-2025 prediction markets (DK/Railbird, FD/CME, dates verified in filings): window ends the month before DK's launch, later months a separate flagged regime. Fields: gross deposits, payouts, net funding (preferred), deposit count, active days, state, DK tenure, pre-period wallet, completeness flags, payroll proxy. Filters: payroll/recurring-bill anchor ≥10 of 12 months; ≥6 pre- and ≥6 post-adoption months; ≥12 for never-adopters; DK-legal states only, rest placebo. Pre/post sports-contract (2025) regimes estimated separately.

**Methodology.** `ΔDK = β·Δshare + α_u + δ_t + Γ'X` with state × month controls; user-clustered SEs, Driscoll–Kraay (4 lags), wild bootstrap (999) over months. Three regressors: raw share (upper bound only); primary `K_t/W̄_u`, W̄ = fixed mean wallet six months pre-first-Kalshi; leave-out share on trailing three-month sportsbook averages. Mechanical null set analytically and by permutation (1,000 within-user DK reshuffles); pass only if β < permuted 5th percentile. `ppmlhdfe` (user, month FE) leads, then by-month 99th-percentile-winsorized OLS, then asinh; never log(x+1). Donut: drop the adoption month, compare t+1..t+6 with t−6..t−1; two DK lags; Arellano–Bond robustness. IV `Z = affinity_u × V_t`, V = national non-sports Kalshi volume, affinity fit on a 50% holdout from pre-period covariates; F > 20, Kleibergen–Paap. Cross-check: Callaway–Sant'Anna event study, first Kalshi use vs not-yet-adopters, matched on pre-period DK decile, tenure, state, pre-trend slope; not TWFE. Credible only if β × mean Δshare matches the ATT within ~2×, else the event study wins. Cuts: tenure <6/6–18/18–36/36+ (only where first DK deposit is ≥3 months post-entry; censored group separate) and pre-first-Kalshi (DK+FD) deciles, never contemporaneous; never-adopters get pseudo-adoption dates from matched strata; pooled plus ten splits, agreement shown. Headline $ displaced per 10-point share rise, not an elasticity; PPML log-log elasticity separately.

**Validation** (kill criteria pre-set). Permutation null: fails if β is indistinguishable at 5%. Placebos: grocery/streaming/general-merchandise outcomes ≈ 0; non-legal states = error floor; neobank/P2P first funding as fake treatment. Pre-trends: six leads jointly insignificant, p > 0.10. FD symmetry, difference = brand switching. Wallet accounting ±6 months: ΔKalshi, ΔDK, ΔFD, Δother gambling, Δsavings, Δdiscretionary as shares summing to 100. Randomization inference: 1,000 within-user adoption-date reassignments. Panel integrity: ≥95% completeness, drop mid-window card/account switches; >30% move = artifact. Out-of-sample: fit 2024 cohorts, predict 2025 adopters. Power first: MDE = 2.8·sd(ΔDK)/(sd(Δshare)·√N_eff), design effect 1+(m−1)ρ, ρ ≈ 0.05–0.15, in $/10 share points.

**Pitfalls.** Denominator: regressor contains the outcome; test against the permuted null, lead with baseline-deflated. Gross deposits ≠ spend: prefer net funding; Kalshi round-trips capital, so compare deposit dollars only. Deposits ≠ revenue: ~9–11% hold vs a small exchange fee; convert at explicit take rates. User-specific seasonality (NFL-only vs year-round, adoption clustered on elections/events): add user-type × season controls. Common-cause shocks (state taxes, levies, promos, outages): state × month and the instrument cover some; a coincident national DK shock is inseparable, flag it. Selection: bound if pre-trends fail. Attrition: balanced and unbalanced panels, Lee bound if they differ. Whales: dollar- and user-weighted separately, headlined if the top decile dominates.

---

PLAN B:

**Hypothesis.** Event contracts partially substitute for sportsbook wagering: Kalshi adopters spend less on DraftKings than absent adoption; null is pure additivity. Estimand: adopters vs their own counterfactual, six post-adoption months. Outcomes: DraftKings gross deposit dollars per user-month (debits, excluding payouts); retention (any DraftKings debit ≥$5 that month); deposit transaction count. Secondary: net dollars, DraftKings wallet share, total wallet including Kalshi, time to 90-day lapse.

**Data.** User-month panel, Jan 2022–latest complete month, card/ACH, one row per user-month, zeros explicit. Taxonomy (one week): DraftKings (`DRAFTKINGS`, `DK*`, sportsbook/casino/DFS, acquirer-prefixed variants, Golden Nugget Online), Kalshi (`KALSHI`, `KALSHIEX`, `KALSHI EXCHANGE LLC`, debit-card/crypto on-ramps), plus FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, ESPN Bet, Fanatics, PrizePicks, Underdog, Polymarket, PredictIt, Robinhood, Interactive Brokers, major crypto exchanges — hand-label top 300 descriptors by volume, 200 random tail strings; coverage target >99% of dollars. Debits/credits separate from ingest; payouts never netted into deposits. Per user: tenure, monthly inflow (income proxy), state, age bucket, panel-activity flag (≥5 monthly transactions of any type/merchant).

**Methodology.** Treatment: first Kalshi debit ≥$5; cohort = that month; six months either side. Eligibility: ≥2 DraftKings-debit months, ≥$50 cumulative in −6..−1; adopters without pre-period DraftKings activity counted separately. Controls: no in-window Kalshi debit, same DraftKings baseline, no Polymarket/PredictIt/other event-venue debit; separate >12-months-later adopter pool, robustness only. Match 1:5 within cohort — exact on state, DraftKings tenure bucket (<6, 6–12, 13–24, 25+ months); nearest-neighbor on a within-cohort logit propensity score over log mean monthly DraftKings dollars, active months, mean transaction count, monthly-dollar OLS slope (−6..−1), log inflow, age bucket, pre-period FanDuel/BetMGM/Caesars/ESPN Bet/DFS indicators, crypto-exchange indicator, Sept–Jan share of DraftKings dollars (sports tilt); with replacement, caliper 0.2 SD linearized score, dropped adopters reported. Stack on (user, cohort, event month), balanced −6..+6, original user id retained.

Estimate $y_{igt}=\sum_{k=-6,k\neq-1}^{6}\beta_k\mathbb{1}[\text{adopter}_i]\mathbb{1}[e_{it}=k]+\alpha_{ig}+\lambda_{g\tau}+\varepsilon_{igt}$: user-by-cohort, cohort-by-calendar-month fixed effects; −1 omitted. Weight controls 1/5, cohorts by adopter count; report cohort weights. Cluster on original user id; under 30 cohorts, add wild cluster bootstrap over cohorts. Pre-committed forms: primary OLS on dollars winsorized at 99th percentile within cohort-by-calendar-month; retention as linear probability model; Poisson PML, same fixed effects, on raw dollars and counts; no $\log(1+y)$ headline. Aggregate $\beta_0$–$\beta_6$ by delta method, reporting +1..+3 and +4..+6 separately. Precompute 80%-power MDE $\approx2.8s\sqrt{(1/N_T+1/5N_T)/7}$; above 5% of baseline mean spend, say so and widen window or pool cohorts.

**Validation.** Standardized mean differences before/after matching, matching covariates plus held-out FanDuel dollars, total discretionary spend, weekend transaction share; threshold 0.10. Raw event-time plot parallel over −6..−1. Joint F-test $\beta_{-6}{=}\cdots{=}\beta_{-2}{=}0$, with power against the claimed post effect. Partial identification: post trend up to $M{\times}$ largest pre-period one; report breakdown $M$ where confidence set includes zero ($M{=}0.5$ is not a result). Placebos: grocery/fuel/e-commerce outcomes; pseudo-adoption six months earlier for then-inactive adopters; first transaction at an unrelated new merchant of comparable ticket, matched identically; whole pipeline on FanDuel-only non-DraftKings users. Robustness: not-yet-adopters as controls; 1:1 matching; no winsorization; drop Nov 2024, Jan 2025 cohorts; exclude adopters already down >50% in −3..−1 vs −6..−4; restrict to users panel-active thirteen months.

**Pitfalls.** Attrition mistaken for churn: condition on panel-activity flag, report adoption's effect on total observed spend, present retention with panel exit coded missing and as zero; if these disagree, headline deposit dollars among panel-active users. Reverse causality from DraftKings limits/self-exclusion: pre-trend test, −3..−1 fade exclusion, strata by whether deposits were already shrinking (both reported). Differential seasonality: cohort-by-calendar effects, matching on sports tilt, wallet share as ratio cross-check. Dollars observed, not trades: sports-specific substitution is inference, not measurement. Joint/shared accounts, multiple cards per person, PayPal-routed deposits attenuate spend; flag in coverage audit. Freeze specification, matching variables, outcome definitions, robustness list in writing; build and validate matching on pre-period data only.
