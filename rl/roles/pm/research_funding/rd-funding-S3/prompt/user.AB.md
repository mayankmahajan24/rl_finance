Who this is for:

A mid-size long/short equity fund that has just licensed a European retail transaction panel. ~30 investment staff, a three-person data team that cleans alt-data panels, and one engineer. Horizon is six to eighteen months. They want to take positions in specific listed European banks and to use the panel to underwrite them — deposit flows, salary capture, customer acquisition, attrition. The obstacle is that the panel's institution IDs are anonymized: until an ID is matched to a bank, none of the behavioral data can be attached to a ticker. What kills the work is a confident wrong match, because the desk would then be sizing a position on another bank's customers. Must be defensible to a PM who will ask what would have to be true for it to be wrong.

Research question:

Can we reliably identify European financial institutions from transaction data, using only anonymized institution IDs plus the demographics and spend behavior of a panel of customers?

---

PLAN A:

**Hypothesis.** IDs profiled by panel-customer behavior and demographics form (H1) sharply separated clusters, (H2) stable across customer resamples and six-month windows, (H3) axes reading as country, charter, business model, franchise, enough to identify. Null: IDs differ only by size and demographic mix. Ladder: country; archetype (neobank, universal incumbent, savings/mortgage specialist, cooperative/mutual, private/wealth, card monoline, e-money wallet/PSP); named institution via calibrated escalation rule.

**Data.** Ledger + roster. Per customer: institution ID, open date, account-type flag, currency, country; age, gender, income-band, postcode-district/NUTS-3, household-size; 24 months of transactions: day/hour timestamp, signed amount (original/EUR), direction, MCC, channel (card-present/CNP/ATM/direct-debit/standing-order/instant-transfer/SEPA-credit-transfer/cheque), card network incl. domestic schemes (Cartes Bancaires, girocard, Bancontact, Dankort, BankAxept, Multibanco, Bizum), stable merchant/counterparty IDs, two-legged-transfer counterparty institution ID; month-end/daily balance (else reconstructed from anchor). Externally: market structure (deposit/current-account shares by country/institution, founding-year, licence, scheme-issuing membership) + payments habits (POS cash-share, ATM-per-capita, payday convention, instant-payment penetration) — ECB SDW, EBA, national-central-bank reports, scheme lists, SPACE. Drop IDs <200 customers/<30,000 transactions; hold out for scoring.

**Method.** Per-institution aggregates (median, IQR, share-above-threshold): salary (recurring credits: same counterparty, monthly, ±15% over four months, >60th credit pct) — last-two-business-day/25th–28th/1st–3rd/Friday shares, day-of-month entropy, detection rate, median EUR salary; debit/credit/direct-debit/standing-order/instant-transfer/cash-withdrawal shares by volume/count; home-country domestic-scheme, contactless/CNP shares, average ticket; ATM/customer-month, median withdrawal, cash-outflow share, zero-ATM-quarter share; median/90th-pct month-end balance, balance-Gini, sub-EUR-50 months, overdrafts; age mean/IQR, under-30/over-65/top-income shares, NUTS-3 Herfindahl, non-modal-country residence; 12-month-new-account share, tenure, active rate; transfer-matrix net position, panel-institution outflow share, in/out-degree, PageRank, salary-vs-top-up fraction, NMF (k=6–10) co-holding loadings. Log amounts/counts, robust-scale (median/IQR), prune Spearman |ρ|>0.9 keeping interpretable member (60–80 features, 150–400 institutions), PCA to 85% variance (8–14). Cluster thrice: Ward Euclidean k=2–15; GMM full-covariance k=2–15, five restarts, BIC; HDBSCAN min_cluster_size=5/min_samples=3, unassignment allowed. Reconcile via co-association across ~30 partitions, average-linkage on 1−co-association; mean co-association = confidence. UMAP for pictures only. Naming: gradient-boosted classifier on pre-PCA features, permutation importance, per-cluster SMD >0.8; country from currency mix, payday distribution, domestic-scheme share, ATM intensity vs habits table (3 of 4 matching, else cross-border); archetype by pre-committed rubric — neobank: under-30 >35%, new accounts >25%, ATM outflow <5%; card monoline: credit-card outflow >80%, salary detection <10%; private bank: median balance >sample 95th pct, over-65 >30%; retail incumbent: salary detection >55%, >3 direct debits/customer-month. Names only via constrained assignment: per country-archetype cell, rank IDs by panel customers, institutions by market share; Hungarian on rank distance; accept only if ≤3 candidates, ranks agree, top-to-runner-up gap clears preset margin. Tiers: A country+archetype, B named dominant, C named candidate set.

**Validation** (gates). Silhouette (mean, per-cluster) + Calinski-Harabasz: <0.25 = no, 0.25–0.40 archetype only, >0.40 country+archetype; negative-median clusters dissolved, members unassigned. Hennig cluster-wise bootstrap, 500 iterations resampling customers (not institutions), refeaturing/re-clustering: max Jaccard >0.75 stable, 0.60–0.75 provisional, <0.60 dissolved; per-institution majority-retention fraction governs naming. Four non-overlapping six-month windows clustered independently: ARI >0.60 supports, <0.40 = period noise; if only December/August collapse, seasonally adjust timing features. Null: 100 permutations reassigning customers at fixed institution size; silhouette must beat 99th percentile. Hand-read 20 random customers' twelve-month narratives per top-five cluster; verdict before signature.

**Pitfalls.** Cohesion isn't correctness: likely wrong target is account role (primary/secondary/savings) — report role-purity beside silhouette; re-cluster on primary accounts if role-homogeneous but institution-heterogeneous. Panel skews young/urban/digital/multi-banked, inflating neobanks, under-representing cooperative/regional banks: use panel-reweighted shares; state bias. An ID may be a BIN range, aggregator endpoint, sub-brand, sponsor-licence white-label — check near-identical feature vectors, never-overlapping customer sets with identical behavior. Confirmatory naming: timestamped rubric plus tiers. If customer count correlates >0.4 with distance-to-centroid, residualize on log count. Country dominates leading components: re-run within-country against own distribution (rung 2). If null unbeaten, Jaccard <0.6 or ARI <0.4, report the negative: country and rough business-model separation only.

PLAN B:

**Hypothesis.** Opaque institution IDs in a European retail panel are identifiable: the institution→fingerprint map is injective for much of the market, more in concentrated (NL, SE) than fragmented markets (DE, AT, IT). Null: fingerprints overlap (350 Sparkassen, Spanish ex-cajas), identification being threshold-fitted noise. Criterion fixed in advance: share of panel deposit volume assigned at ≥95% precision on held-out institutions; below 40% the country is not identifiable.

**Registers.** `registers.yaml` splits variables into A (panel inputs) and B (anchors — scoring only, never feature building, weighting, calibration or selection); CI fails if B reaches features.

**Data.** Panel, ≥18 months per country: customer ID, provider and counterparty institution IDs, daily timestamp, signed amount, MCC, merchant name, transaction type; age, sex, income band, NUTS-3 postcode. ≥30,000 customers per country, 100,000+ Germany. Anchors: ECB RIAD/MFI register (household-deposit holders, LEI-joined, quarter-versioned); deposit share — ECB CBD2, Bundesbank, DNB, Banco de España, Banca d'Italia, ACPR/Banque de France, KNF, EBA Transparency Exercise; footprints — ECB Payments Statistics, OSM `amenity=bank`/`atm` operator/brand tags via Overpass at NUTS-3 (coverage as known missingness), branch directories; tariffs — *Preis- und Leistungsverzeichnis*, tarievenoverzicht, *folleto de tarifas*; benefit calendars — Kindergeld by Kindergeldnummer digit, AOW/toeslagen, CAF, Seguridad Social, INPS; growth disclosures — N26, Revolut, bunq, Nickel, ING, incumbents.

**Method.** Fingerprints per country/ID/quarter: `n_share` (raked to Eurostat NUTS-2 × age × sex × income-decile margins), `geo` (Laplace-smoothed NUTS-3 shares), `age_dist`/`inc_dist`, `payday` (plus salary-only: recurring, same counterparty, ≥60% of inflow), `benefit_mix`, `fee_signature` (modal recurring debit, cents), `scheme_mix`, `growth`, `atm_geo`. Cluster IDs sharing customers, fee signature, geography; roster group → legal entity → brand. `S[i,r]` sums log-likelihood ratios over size (`log10(n_share/deposit_share)`, Gaussian, μ_c absorbing panel skew, σ_c trained), geography (Jensen–Shannon divergence vs branch footprint, logistic-mapped), fee (exact-match bonus, heavy contradiction penalty), demographics (Wasserstein vs published profiles or type prior), growth correlation, timing. Assign jointly: min-cost flow, per-ID abstain sink at τ_c, capacities >1 for multi-ID institutions, not-in-roster sink; point estimate by Jonker–Volgenant (`scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment`, augmented matrix); marginals by Sinkhorn (ε matched to a Gibbs sampler's entropy on a small country) or Metropolis. Accept only if `max p ≥ 0.80`, margin ≥ 0.25, ≥3 comparators favouring, ≥300 customers that quarter; else `UNKNOWN` (`insufficient_evidence`, `degenerate_class`, `no_roster_candidate`, `anchor_conflict`). Thresholds recalibrated by Venn–Abers or split-conformal at 95% precision.

**Validation.** Labels: consented survey (500 respondents/country), provider-revealed seeds, unambiguous public cases; 25–60 per country, below 15 unvalidatable. Holdout unit is the institution: leave-one-institution-out, refitting weights, μ_c, σ_c, geography logistic and calibration in-fold, roster row still present (nested CV). Then leave-one-country-out: fit four, apply to the fifth on its own anchors; report both and the gap. Metrics: precision, recall, coverage unweighted and deposit-volume-weighted, risk–coverage curve, top-3 accuracy, type-level confusion. Pilots: Netherlands (if it fails there, stop), Germany, Spain, France, Poland. Controls first: permuted fingerprints must score at chance; three phantom IDs must abstain; dropping the largest comparator must degrade gracefully.

**Pitfalls.** Vendor shares themselves panel-derived — regulator/institution primaries, documented provenance, no undisclosed methodologies. Panel skew read as noise — per-type selection term, post-stratification, drop size comparator if residual skew unstable. Anchor vintage amid consolidation (CaixaBank/Bankia, Unicaja/Liberbank, mutual mergers) — quarterly versioning, temporal joins, mergers as roster transitions. Degeneracy — abstain with a candidate set from near-tie Sinkhorn marginals; class size reported. Truth outside the roster (BaaS sponsors, passported entities) — keep the sink, priored from e-money share. Tourism/commuting distortion (Balearics, Côte d'Azur, Tyrol, borders) — downweight those NUTS-3 regions. Correlated timing features — comparator covariance, multivariate scoring. Threshold overfitting — nested CV, bootstrap over institutions not transactions. Governance is gate zero: licence and GDPR clearance, no individual-level output, minimum cells (300 customers per institution, 50 per geography cell), agreed crosswalk access; if forbidden, stop.
