Who this is for:

A single-name event-driven desk inside a multi-manager platform. One PM, two analysts. Horizon is weeks to a few months around a catalyst. Deep primary-source and legal-document work, expert networks, and a risk manager who cuts positions fast. No quantitative research staff and no capacity to build datasets; they consume sell-side, filings, and expert calls. The question is always whether this specific situation is mispriced right now, not whether an effect holds on average.

Research question:

Sell-side channel checks are incrementally positive on new logo wins. Should we expect a meaningful boost to full-year guidance at the upcoming print, and is this a long?

---

PLAN A:

The channel checks are already public via sell-side notes, so the question isn't whether new logo wins are real but what's left to earn. Two-stage, gated plan: stage one measures how much check-driven optimism is already in consensus and price; only if a gap survives do we ask whether management will put it in the guide.

**Hypothesis (falsifiable):** New logo momentum is real but (a) already reflected in consensus revenue and relative stock performance since the notes went out, and (b) unlikely to convert into a surprising full-year raise, since management's policy is to hold/nudge the full year at this point in the fiscal calendar. Both legs must be wrong for a long case.

**Stage one — is it priced?** Build a dated event log (broker portals/sales coverage, Bloomberg RES/ANR/EE/CN, Refinitiv/Visible Alpha) to find the "information date" — first wide circulation of the check thesis, not necessarily the first note. Measure consensus revision from information date minus 5 trading days to today: change in mean current-quarter and full-year revenue/EPS, dispersion/contributor count, and a "whisper proxy" (last-15-day estimates vs. stale full consensus). Thresholds: >~0.5% full-year move plus recent-updater subset >1% above stale mean means priced as expected; <0.2% move means checks were noted and dismissed. Run an event study: 250-day estimation window ending 30 days prior, two-factor model (index + equal-weighted basket of 6-10 close comps chosen pre-results), CAR over (-1,+1), (0,+10), (0,today), with standard errors and dollar market cap. Translate CAR into implied fundamentals: back out implied revenue/ARR at constant multiple, compare to what checks can arithmetically deliver (logo count × defensible ACV × fraction landing this fiscal year given ramp lag). Gate to stage two only if full-year consensus revision is under ~0.5%, (0,today) CAR is under ~1 estimation-window SD and under the arithmetic-implied CAR, and straddle-implied earnings move is not >~1.2x trailing 8-quarter realized median. Two of three failing means stop and write the pass note.

**Stage two — will guidance move?** Build a guidance history table over 12-16 quarters (or full CFO tenure) from 10-Qs, 8-Ks, transcripts: guided range, actual, beat size, raised/held/cut, and the raise-to-beat pass-through ratio, plus guidance-philosophy language. Extract the policy: base rate of full-year raises at this specific fiscal quarter, median pass-through, and whether it shifted with a CFO change. Check whether the mechanism reaches the guide: signature-to-revenue-recognition lag from RPO/deferred revenue disclosures, and size new logos against the base (may sit inside NRR/churn noise). Model three scenarios (no change, token pass-through raise, above-beat raise) weighted by the base rates; estimate reaction via regression of historical earnings-day CAR on guidance revision size. Require materially positive expected value, sized against the straddle.

**Validation:** reproduce consensus via two vendors; re-run the event study with a different comp basket and a plain market model; have a second person re-extract three random guidance-table quarters (watch for restated vs. as-given figures); backtest "never raises here" on the two most recent instances; check prior check episodes' actual outcomes; seek disconfirming views from analysts who didn't raise, and check pricing pressure via revenue-per-customer.

**Pitfalls:** checks are a non-random, undersized sample with no denominator — directional only. CAR must be measured against comps/factors, not raw moves. Watch the reverse sandbag trap (expected conservatism already priced). Log confounding CAR-window events. Fix comp basket and quarter count before the guidance backtest to avoid selection bias. The 12-16 quarter base rate has a wide confidence interval — report as such. Three of four outcome states are bad (priced checks; right checks, no raise; wrong checks), hence the default is no. Clear sourcing with compliance first — former-employee/expert-network checks can shade into MNPI.

---

PLAN B:

Separate two questions: whether new-logo upside moves in-year revenue enough to shift a policy-set guide, versus whether it matters to a twelve-month ARR thesis.

Hypothesis: incremental new-logo activity, at face value, adds under 75bps to fiscal-year revenue vs. guidance midpoint — inside the noise band and below management's bar for raising beyond simple beat flow-through — so the print isn't the catalyst and any long rests on next-year ARR. Alternate: if revenue is consumption-weighted or lands are large/fast-ramping, in-year sensitivity is far higher and the trade is live. Output: implied FY revenue in bps vs. midpoint and consensus, plus exiting ARR and next-year revenue.

Data: 12 10-Qs, 3 10-Ks, ARR/RPO/cRPO disclosures, customer counts (total/>$100k), deferred revenue/unbilled receivables, net/gross retention, services revenue/segment splits, the S-1/investor-day cohort chart of expansion by signing year, and 3 years of transcripts tagged for guidance language. Consensus: Visible Alpha line items, sell-side models from three most-read analysts, 30/90-day revision trends, buy-side bogey vs. printed consensus. Channel: 4-6 expert calls (two former quota-carrying sales leaders, two partners/SIs incl. one losing share, one procurement buyer) for counts — registrations YoY, close rates/ACV/discounting, implementation start dates. Alternative: LinkedIn/job-posting headcount (reps, implementation/PS staff), cloud-marketplace listings, G2 velocity, HG Insights/Enlyft. Market: front-expiry implied move, realized moves over 8 prints, short interest/borrow, stock move since notes published.

Methodology: reconstruct 12 quarters' revenue bottom-up (installed base + churn/expansion + trailing-8Q cohort + services), tying within ~1%. Solve initial land value: net-new ARR minus retention-implied expansion/churn, over logo adds; trend it. Calibrate ramp curve R(k): R(0) ≈ max(0,(45−L)/90) for lag L; seed R(4)–R(12) from cohort chart; fit L and expansion path via least-squares to actuals, bounded by expert calls. Shock: turn commentary into a logo delta via a chain (partner-sourced share only, registration-to-close at historical rate), 0.5x haircut base case (full credit bull), land value at trailing average and -25%. Run Monte Carlo over logo delta, land value, lag, close rate; report p10/50/90 for FY revenue bps, exiting ARR, and next-FY revenue. Separately, tabulate guided-vs-actual flow-through; ~1.0x Q2 flow-through shifts burden of proof against the thesis.

Validation: backtest 8 prior quarters — compare contemporaneous channel commentary to actual guidance; positive checks 6/8 times but raises only 3/8: the signal is uninformative — stop. Cross-check implied bookings vs. cRPO growth (flag if acceleration is highest-ever); new-logo ARR per ramped rep (hired >2Q ago) vs. its 3-year distribution (haircut above the 90th percentile); and services revenue/implementation headcount accelerating a quarter ahead of claimed bookings. Check whether the market already owns it: stock move, revision direction, implied vs. realized move.

Pitfalls: retention dominating logo count as the in-year driver; logo surges paired with falling land size netting flat or negative; conflating registered/closed/live deals and unchecked partner-incentive changes; guidance as a management decision (CFO tenure, macro caution, M&A) overriding arithmetic; structural breaks — prepaid multi-year contracts, ASC 606 lumpy term licenses, disclosure changes, consumption pricing shortening the lag; reflexivity — the market may already have priced the notes, so model reaction against whisper, not consensus.

Position: map guidance outcomes to price via regression of 8 prior post-earnings returns against forward-estimate changes; expected move must exceed implied move by ≥1.2x. Take a position only if FY revenue is ≥75bps above midpoint, ≥50bps above consensus, raise exceeds 1.5x median Q2 raise, and capacity/cRPO checks pass — else skip the print. Size off loss: ~12% downside scenario, 50bps NAV risk budget ≈ 4% NAV. Stop on cRPO deceleration, weak customer adds, a raise at/below flow-through, or falling land value; 8% price stop; flat within two sessions post-print. Record the twelve-month case as a post-event long if numbers support it.
