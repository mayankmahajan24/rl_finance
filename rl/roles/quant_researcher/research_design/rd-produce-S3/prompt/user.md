Who this is for:

A mid-size long/short equity fund that has just licensed a European retail transaction panel. ~30 investment staff, a three-person data team that cleans alt-data panels, and one engineer. Horizon is six to eighteen months. They want to take positions in specific listed European banks and to use the panel to underwrite them — deposit flows, salary capture, customer acquisition, attrition. The obstacle is that the panel's institution IDs are anonymized: until an ID is matched to a bank, none of the behavioral data can be attached to a ticker. What kills the work is a confident wrong match, because the desk would then be sizing a position on another bank's customers. Must be defensible to a PM who will ask what would have to be true for it to be wrong.

Here is the research idea:

Can we reliably identify European financial institutions from transaction data, using only anonymized institution IDs plus the demographics and spend behavior of a panel of customers?

Write the research plan.
