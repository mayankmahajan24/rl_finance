# Grader review: anomaly_hard (commit f6c54b3)

**Verdict:** not sound as it stands (findings 1-4 are FAILs). Review only; nothing was changed. Weights, gates and
what the task measures are the owner's call.

Method: an independent reader compared instruction.md, build.py, perturb.py, grade.py, writeup.py and judge.md, and
passed synthetic outputs built from the gold through the grader's scoring functions on train 001-004 (12 panels). No
score.py, no stability run and no judge were executed for these numbers.

## Findings, most serious first

1. **The false-alarm factor is avoided by never scoring a kept row under 0.5** (`grade.py` `prec = ... else 1.0`).
   Same cleaning: honest low confidence on rows not yet repaired 0.932; those rows at 0.50-0.56 0.930; honest plus 30
   single-day false alarms (0.15% of rows) 0.789. An alert on a row that was detected and repaired well counts as
   false. The instruction says "below 0.5 means do not [trust]" and "a problem not yet caught should carry low
   confidence". sister_gap raises no alerts; the fresh Opus runs have alert precision 0.07-0.23.
2. **The continuity gate zeroes a calibrated two-level answer and is passed by adding jitter**; a constant 1.0 passes.
3. **A judge failure pays the full report multiplier** (writeup_score None -> data reward x 1.0); a timeout crashes.
4. **Point-in-time cutoffs are fixed at 45% and 70% of the span**, so look-ahead on the last 30% is not caught.
5. **Equal weight per kind**: stuck (~8 rows a panel), intermittent (~17), multi-day (~20), one-day (~20) carry half
   of each part; the whole-history swap (~1800 rows, a three-line rule) carries an eighth. Stuck feeds are not
   described in the instruction.
6. **Kind labels leak** in the confidence part: a one-day problem's 30-day aftermath relabels the level change around
   it; the cleaned part uses each event's own span.
7. **Stability is 1.0 for anything that raises no alerts**, so 10% is free (do-nothing data reward 0.10).
8. **False repairs are nearly free in the cleaned part**: re-imputing 10% of healthy rows costs 0.032.
9. **"Visible" and "wrong" use different bars**: day one of ramps and slow shifts is usually too close to call, so the
   heaviest early day is often unscored; early weighting exists only in the confidence part.
10. **The confidence part mostly re-measures cleaning**: good cleaning with constant confidence 1.0 scores 0.754 on
    confidence, 0.867 overall, against 0.898 and 0.932 with honest confidence.
11. **Instruction mismatches**: whole-history swap vs "its own history"; slow ramps (README: cleaned only; grader:
    both); thin-after-established rows (instruction: cleaned only; grader: both); "small slow drift" does not exist in
    the data; controls skip the false-alarm factor; undisclosed aftermath, alert and right/wrong bars; timeouts crash.
12. **Report**: 18 of 42 equal items are form; validation numbers are unchecked; the multiplier is 0.5-1.0.
13. **Stale text and duplicated constants** in grade.py (module docstring, three definitions of an alert and of
    noise, unused gold fields, the 0.5 and twenty-a-day constants in several places).
