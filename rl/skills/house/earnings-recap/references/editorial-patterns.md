## Guide quality matters more than the headline raise

SOFI reported Q2’26 earnings this AM and is -11% despite a headline beat / revenue raise (adj. rev $1.206B vs. $1.116B Street, adj. EBITDA $358M vs. $336M and adj. EPS $0.12 vs. $0.11), with FY EBITDA / EPS guidance unchanged. SOFI came into the print with a beat / raise expected after mgmt left FY guidance untouched in Q1, and the market needed to see capital-light growth with real earnings flow-through. Instead, the upside was concentrated in balance-sheet-heavy Lending while LPB was only roughly in line, Financial Services missed, non-interest expense ran 8% above Street with CET1 falling 240bps q/q. Importantly, SOFI raised the revenue midpoint by ~$145M without raising EBITDA / EPS and now needs ~36% H2 EBITDA margins vs. 29.7% in Q2

## A live disruption debate can outrank a routine beat

**ADP printed this AM and stock is +3% after a modest Q4’26 beat and initial FY27 guide that was in line with street on revenue and margin / EPS. The stock was flat pre-market, then moved up as mgmt framed AI as a bookings, retention and productivity driver, NOT a payroll / HCM disruptor. Investors cared about FY27 revenue growth not going away and a base 70-90bps FY27 margin guide with a path to faster AI-driven margin delivery, while bookings / pricing / retention defended that the core business is not rolling over. We think this is part of a broader SW theme of companies like ADP ($110B MC | +8% YTD | 22x NTM PE) / PAYX ($43B MC | +12% YTD | 20x NTM PE) moving from “AI-loser” to “AI-safe” -> if SoRs can prove their business is not being replaced by AI-natives or the labs, stock moves up on multiple expansion after being crushed this year.**

## Segment mix can reverse the meaning of a consolidated raise

Stock -8% intraday despite beat on Revenue and EPS and F2Q27 guidance moderately above consensus (along with raised full-year guide). FLEX’s CPI business is a clear 800V DC winner --> opportunity here is that this Tier-1 AI power + cooling business is hiding inside a low-multiple EMS shell, and will be spun off in calendar 1Q'27. ## A leading indicator can decide the revenue outlook

DOCU -19% pre-market on weak earnings. Biggest issue is DOCU’s billings growth (fwd indicator) decelerated from +11% last Q to +4% this Q, missing guide by ~1pt and street by ~2pts. This means investor hopes for acceleration in subscription revenue growth from HSD to LDD are unlikely to play out near-term, and DOCU cut their full-year billings guide by ~2pts in conjunction with the miss. Mgmt blamed the miss on lower-than-expected early renewals due to recent GTM changes, assigning new territory coverage & customer segmentation for sales reps. But this is a major disappointment after the stock outperformed the IGV by ~40pts over the last year in hopes of a reacceleration of the top-line. After the move, stock trades for ~21x/19x ‘25/’26 Adj. P/E which is fair value for HSD Revs growth / LDD EPS growth.

## Organic growth can matter more than acquisition-driven guidance

PAYX -9% on weak earnings. Missed slightly on FQ4 total revs growth but biggest problem is they disclosed the organic revenue is growing +3%, well below prior disclosures of +6-7%. Guidance for next FY of +16.5-18.5% growth also fell short of street expectations of +18% and requires the organic revenue to accelerate from +3% to +5% growth. Management blamed the weak results on macro, citing an uptick in uncertainty / slower decision making, as well as an increase in bankruptcies among SMBs.
