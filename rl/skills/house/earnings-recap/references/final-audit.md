# Earnings recap final audit

Read the completed draft against the source, ledger, and strongest contrary evidence.
Return it only after one private revision.

## Source audit

- Verify every number, comparison, directional word, calculation, attribution, and
  quote in its exact source context.
- Preserve entity, segment, period, unit, denominator, status, and comparator. Match
  scopes in actual-versus-expectation and guidance comparisons.
- For every selected guide field, preserve its most useful sourced comparator. Do not
  describe a guide as flat sequentially when its change versus expectations points in
  the other direction.
- Reconcile each value against the source appropriate to that fact: company materials
  for reported metrics, the complete transcript for management language, and a dated
  external source for Street expectations or market reaction. Omit or attribute a
  difference that remains unresolved.
- Distinguish reported facts, management framing, external expectations, house context,
  and inference. Keep analyst premises and conditional answers labeled.
- Preserve quote wording, speaker role, context, and material qualifiers. Narrow any
  claim that is stronger than its source.

## Decision audit

- Does the opening carry the most important surprise or forward change and strongest
  counterweight?
- When a dated numeric stock reaction was found, does the opening state its magnitude
  and distinguish sourced explanation from inference?
- Does every body conclusion add a distinct estimate, thesis, catalyst, risk, or
  portfolio decision beyond the opening?
- Did the strongest omitted company or portfolio decision beat the weakest included
  theme, or is its exclusion defensible?
- Are acquisition, price, mix, currency, accounting, temporary items, and capital
  intensity treated correctly when they change earnings quality?
- Does the central debate preserve the strongest adverse evidence and the next proof
  point? Does every strategic opportunity preserve status, economics, timing, and the
  path to proof?
- Where linked volume and value measures permit it, did the draft test the headline
  growth claim with a per-unit price, mix, or monetization bridge?
- Did a distant opportunity or one-time accounting item displace a nearer recurring
  revenue or earnings ramp supported by adoption, orders, design wins, or commitments?
- Does the post resolve the central bull/bear tradeoff into a present estimate or
  thesis decision before listing proof points to monitor?
- Does each implication add a supported consequence with a clear mechanism and the
  most relevant exposure?
- Did two target-company implications displace a supported cross-position consequence
  from a top-ranked conclusion? If so, replace the weaker repetition after checking
  relative exposure.
- Did a narrow supporting fact displace a sourced company-wide framework change that
  matters more to multi-year underwriting?
- Where disclosed inputs permit a useful organic or per-unit range, did the draft do
  the calculation, label its assumptions, and carry the resulting decision?
- Did related strategic developments that share a monetization path receive one
  combined business-model decision rather than losing separately as minor details?
- When `Implications` is included, does it add a target action or cross-position
  conclusion absent from the body, rather than restating a selected theme?

## Publication audit

- Follow [the Slack format](slack-format.md), including real Markdown bold, literal
  bullet characters, indentation, and `Implications` only when useful.
- Keep the opening to at most three sentences and below 90 words. Aim for 375-450 words
  and never exceed 475.
- Remove repetition, routine scorecard detail, product tours, generic background,
  unsupported optionality, and implications that restate the body.
- Return no title, citations, links, methodology, confidence labels, or source footer
  unless the user requested them.
