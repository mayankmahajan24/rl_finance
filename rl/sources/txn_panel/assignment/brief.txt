Card-transaction sensor panel
Overview
This is a small, partly simplified subset of a daily card-transaction panel. For each company or company segment it counts the number of transactions and the dollar amount spent per day, as reported by four anonymised data sources. Treat each source as a "sensor" that gives a timely read on the underlying company's trends. Sensors sometimes malfunction and give poor reads.
Known issues with the data
Anomalous values: a single datapoint can be too low when a sensor fails to transmit, or too high for a variety of reasons (double counting, contamination and so on).
Level shifts: when a sensor changes how it behaves, its read can move persistently higher or lower, either for a period or permanently.
There is no ground truth. Past history and comparisons across series are the only guide to what is normal.
Columns
date: the day the transactions occurred
ct: the number of transactions the sensor reported
amt: the total dollar value of those transactions
ticker: a company, or a company segment when the ticker contains an underscore
industry: the company's industry; only two of many are included
source_id: a unique id for each sensor; only four are included
